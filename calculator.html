About the Formulas

Riegel Formula — T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06. Pete Riegel's fatigue coefficient accurately predicts road race performance. Less accurate beyond 100 miles or for highly technical trail courses.

VO2 Max (Daniels) — Estimated from velocity and oxygen cost at race pace. A rough indicator — lab testing gives a true figure.

Trail Note — For mountain ultras, add 10-20% to predicted times to account for elevation and terrain.

How to Use Your Zones

Zone 1-2 — Easy aerobic. 70-80% of your weekly training. Builds base, improves fat burning, aids recovery.

Zone 3 — Tempo. Comfortably hard. Use sparingly - 1 session per week max.

Zone 4 — Threshold. Race specific intensity. Hard interval sessions.

Zone 5 — VO2 max. Maximum effort. Short reps only, full recovery between.
